## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a tranquil, dignified, and tactile sanctuary for daily Islamic observance. Rooted in the Quranic concept of *Sakīnah* (deep spiritual serenity, reassurance, and peace of heart), the visual voice avoids ostentatious ornamentation and cluttered interfaces. Instead, it pairs contemporary editorial discipline with understated sacred geometry, natural materiality, and calm intentionality.

### Design Principles
- **Spiritual Quietude**: Interfaces emphasize breathing room, deliberate cadence, and reduction of cognitive friction. The app is a quiet pause in a loud day.
- **Organic Earth & Sanctuary**: Textures evocative of linen, smooth stone, sage foliage, deep forest sanctuary, and matte beaten gold provide an organic, non-synthetic aesthetic.
- **Quiet Precision**: Interaction patterns reflect the high craft of contemporary mobile operating systems: smooth spring physics, subtle border definitions, and gentle haptics.
- **Dual-Script Equilibrium**: English and Arabic typographic hierarchies exist in proportional harmony, respecting natural RTL/LTR dynamics, sacred calligraphic proportions, and readability in low-light prayer environments.

## Colors

The color palette translates sacred natural elements into a calibrated color system optimized for day and night prayer contexts.

### Light Mode Roles
- **Canvas (`#F8F7F3`)**: Calm linen/cream. Softens eye strain compared to pure white, establishing a warm, grounded foundation.
- **Primary / Dominant Accent (`#183C35`)**: Deep spiritual forest green. Used for major structural anchors, primary CTAs, active states, and focal headers.
- **Secondary / Subtle Tone (`#6F8178`)**: Sage slate. Serves as supporting iconography color, secondary tags, inactive indicators, and soft dividers.
- **Tertiary / Sacred Accent (`#C9A86A`)**: Matte sand gold. Used sparingly for spiritual milestones, golden ratio markers, prayer time progress pointers, and Quranic verse verse markers (*Ayah* stops).
- **Surface (`#FFFFFF`)**: Pure white cards and sheets that lift gently off the linen canvas.
- **Text & Foreground (`#202522`)**: Soft charcoal. Offers high contrast readability without the harshness of pure `#000000`.

### Dark Mode Adaptation
- **Dark Canvas (`#111513`)**: Obsidian-tinted peat forest. Maintains deep OLED battery savings while avoiding stark pitch-black.
- **Dark Surface (`#18201D`)**: Slightly elevated pine-slate container tone for cards and bottom navigation.
- **Dark Text (`#E8EEE9`)**: Muted chalk white with gentle warmth.
- **Dark Accent (`#C8A96B`)**: Refined warm bronze gold that prevents glare during Tahajjud or Fajr night readings.

## Typography

The typographic balance bridges clean Latin geometric warmth with fluid Arabic script dynamics.

### Font Role Allocation
- **Latin Display, Body, and UI (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Clean, contemporary geometry with softly curved terminals that mirror our rounded corner visual language. It provides pristine clarity for timestamps, navigation, and English translations.
- **Arabic Quranic Verses (`Noto Naskh Arabic` / `Noto Serif` Fallback)**: Employed specifically for Quran and Hadith recitations. The vertical line-height is intentionally expanded (`52px` line-height for `28px` font size) to allow complex diacritics (*tashkeel* and *harakat*) adequate room without visual collision.
- **Arabic UI Elements (`IBM Plex Sans Arabic`)**: Used for functional RTL navigation, button labels, and system status messages to maintain a structured modern feel.

### Rules of Typesetting
- Avoid pure black `#000000` text in all instances.
- For Quranic text presentation, centralize tracking, disable hyphenation, and keep paragraph widths restricted to an optimal reading measure of max 520px on tablets.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy follows a mobile-first fluid model governed by organic padding and breathing space.

### Spatial Rhythm
- **Base Grid**: Built on an absolute `4px` grid with standard steps at `8px`, `16px`, `24px`, and `32px`.
- **Canvas Margins**: Set to `1.25rem` (20px) on standard mobile devices to keep touch targets safe from bezel gestures, widening to `2rem` (32px) on tablet viewports.
- **Vertical Air**: Key content segments (e.g., between the Prayer Time Carousel and Daily Reflection) maintain a minimum gap of `space-xl` (32px). Cramped layouts are forbidden, as spatial generosity directly triggers the psychological feeling of calmness.
- **Flutter Safe Area Handling**: Floating navigation bars and prayer widgets anchor gracefully to bottom safe areas with added vertical cushion (`space-md`).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy eschews dramatic drop shadows in favor of ambient physical paper-stacking, whisper shadows, and tonal boundaries.

### Depth Mechanics
- **The Whisper Shadow**: Elevation 1 uses a soft, tinted spread: `0px 4px 20px -2px rgba(24, 60, 53, 0.04)`. The shadow is tinted with the primary forest green rather than raw gray, grounding objects naturally into the linen background.
- **Surface Elevation (Cards & Sheets)**: Instead of heavy drop shadows, cards use a 1px boundary stroke `rgba(32, 37, 34, 0.05)` (Light mode) and `rgba(232, 238, 233, 0.08)` (Dark mode) paired with an internal soft gradient fill.
- **Modal Sheets**: Bottom sheets employ Elevation 2: `0px -8px 32px 0px rgba(17, 21, 19, 0.08)` coupled with an ultra-thin gold top stroke (`#C9A86A` at 15% opacity).
- **Interactive State Feedback**: When pressed or tapped, cards translate 1px downward and scale subtly (`0.985`), evoking a soft, tactile cushion response.

## Shapes

The shape system expresses serenity through organic, generous curves reminiscent of traditional Islamic domes, polished river stones, and smooth calligraphy tools.

### Corner Radii Guidelines
- **Primary Content Cards**: Standardized at `rounded-2xl` (`1.25rem` / `20px` in Flutter `BorderRadius.circular(20)`).
- **Hero & Daily Surah Containers**: Set at `rounded-3xl` (`1.75rem` / `28px`) to create an inviting, pillowed canvas.
- **Interactive Controls & Chips**: Buttons, floating prayer pills, and search inputs leverage full continuous curves or `rounded-full` / pill shapes for effortless touchability.
- **Inner Nested Radius Principle**: If a child container sits inside a `24px` card with `12px` padding, the child must carry a radius of `12px` (`24 - 12 = 12`) to preserve concentric aesthetic harmony.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (e.g., "Begin Dhikr", "Log Prayer")**: Solid `#183C35` background, text in `#FFFFFF`, pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), height of 54px with horizontal padding of 28px. Pressed states show a soft shift to `#122D28`.
- **Secondary Action**: Border button with a 1.5px stroke of `#6F8178` at 30% opacity, surface transparent, text `#183C35` (or `#E8EEE9` in dark mode).
- **Sacred / Tertiary Action**: Warm sand gold surface (`#C9A86A`) with deep forest green text (`#183C35`) for moments of spiritual significance.

### Cards & Sanctuary Tiles
- White canvas (`#FFFFFF`) with a 20px corner radius, hairline border `rgba(32, 37, 34, 0.04)`, and whisper shadow.
- Inner card padding is generous (`space-lg` / 24px).
- Internal hierarchy: Category label in small-caps gold (`#C9A86A`), headline in `#202522`, and supporting text in muted slate (`#6F8178`).

### Prayer Time Timeline / Bar
- A smooth horizontal strip of 5 daily prayer nodes.
- **Passed State**: Muted sage text with subtle checkmark.
- **Current / Imminent State**: Encased in an active deep green pill container (`#183C35`) with white text and a shimmering gold countdown ring indicator.
- **Upcoming State**: Muted linen card background with gentle typography.

### Dhikr Counter & Tasbih Ring
- A circular tactile counter utilizing smooth radial progress.
- Primary stroke in `#C9A86A` gold over a muted track `#6F8178` (20% opacity).
- Core counter value rendered in `Plus Jakarta Sans` 48px Semibold.
- Subtle Flutter haptic feedback triggers on each increment (`HapticFeedback.lightImpact()`).

### Quran & Du'a Reader
- Bifold reading layout: Arabic verse centered at top, generous line height (minimum 1.8x), followed by an optional subtle divider line tinted with warm sand (`#C9A86A` at 20%), followed by the English translation in `body-lg` slate.
- Verse numbers enclosed within an ornamental eight-point star or soft circular glyph in gold.

### Inputs & Search Bars
- Background filled with `rgba(111, 129, 120, 0.08)`. No harsh bottom lines; completely enclosed rounded capsule (`rounded-2xl`).
- Placeholder text in `#6F8178` with leading search icon in primary green.
